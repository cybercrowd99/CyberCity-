# CyberCity

## A Digital City Framework

CyberCity is not a collection of websites.

It is a spatial model of the real world where places are the primary units of existence.

CyberCity transforms locations into structured digital places with boundaries, presence, purpose, and participation. Restaurants, stadiums, community spaces, events, local businesses, and temporary activities become living digital representations instead of disconnected pages and listings.

The foundation of CyberCity is:

- **Place before feature**
- **Boundary before capability**
- **Structure before intelligence**
- **Discovery through presence, not pressure**

Every place is modeled as a container that can hold its own organs, services, events, commerce, and community interactions while preserving clear boundaries and non-interference.

CyberCity creates a digital layer for the physical world — a city where people discover places, experiences, and opportunities through movement, proximity, and meaningful participation.
